DroidServer turns an Android phone into a backend for your apps and sites. Everything runs on the phone; no cloud account is needed.

• Data and API: collections (SQLite or PostgreSQL), automatically generated REST and GraphQL APIs, realtime subscriptions, search, import/export, location and maps, calendar (CalDAV) and contacts (CardDAV), offline sync in the client SDKs.
• Users and rules: accounts, roles, teams, two-step verification, passkeys, social login; access rules per collection.
• Files and sites: file fields, thumbnails, video streaming, static site hosting, no-code pages and dashboards, network drive (WebDAV).
• Automation: JavaScript scripts, a job queue, no-code automation rules, forms, webhooks, MQTT (including TLS) and Home Assistant.
• AI: a Claude, OpenAI or Gemini proxy, questions over your records and semantic search; or models on the phone (llama.cpp) with no data leaving it.
• Payments: payment links, subscriptions, refunds and receipts with Stripe, iyzico and Paddle; card details never pass through the phone.
• Packages: share collections, rules, automations, pages and scripts in one file; installing shows what will change first.
• Management: a setup wizard, web panel, command-line tool, backups (optionally encrypted to the cloud), audit log, health alerts (notification, e-mail, Telegram) and Telegram commands.
• Internet access: Cloudflare tunnel, Tailscale, or direct access with your domain and a Let's Encrypt certificate.

Optional side services (PostgreSQL, Redis, Node.js, Python, Java, Cloudflare tunnel) are downloaded from Play as modules and managed from within the app.
