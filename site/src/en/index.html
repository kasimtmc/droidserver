Make your phone a server

DroidServer turns an Android phone into a backend for your apps and sites: the database, API, user accounts and web panel run on the phone. No cloud account needed; your data stays with you.

Get it on Google Play APK from GitHub

Try it free for 7 days, then a one-time payment. No subscription.

What it can do

Data and API

Collections (SQLite or PostgreSQL), automatic REST and GraphQL API, live subscriptions, search, import and export, offline sync.

Users and rules

Accounts, roles, teams, two-step verification, passkeys and social sign-in; who can read and write, per collection.

Sites and files

Static site publishing, no-code pages and dashboards, file fields, thumbnails, video streaming and a network drive (WebDAV).

Automation

JavaScript scripts, a job queue, no-code automation rules, forms, webhooks, and home automation with MQTT and Home Assistant.

AI and payments

Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, or models on the phone. Payment links and subscriptions with Stripe, iyzico and Paddle.

Side services

PostgreSQL, Redis, Cloudflare Tunnel and your own Node.js, Python and Java apps, installed and managed from the app.

Access from the internet

Cloudflare Tunnel, Tailscale, or direct access with your own domain and a Let's Encrypt certificate.

Management

A wizard, a web panel, a command-line tool, backups, an audit log, health alerts and commands from Telegram.

In three steps

  1. Install. From Google Play or GitHub; no root needed.
  2. Choose. Start from a ready-made example (blog, shop, appointments, task tracking) or set up your collections and rules with the wizard.
  3. Start. Connect right away from the same Wi-Fi, or from anywhere through a tunnel.
